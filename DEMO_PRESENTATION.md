## 📽️ SLIDE 1: Title & Team Identity
* **Project Name:** SOL SURVIVOR (AI-Powered Mars Mission Digital Twin & NASA Space Academy)
* **Challenge:** NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026
* **Target Audience:** Students aged 8–16, Educators & STEM Classrooms
* **Core Vision:** Transforming real NASA planetary exploration, closed-loop life-support physics, and astrodynamics into an immersive, gamified flight control center.
* **Tagline:** *"Stealth Learning on the Martian Frontier."*

---

## ⚠️ SLIDE 2: The Problem – The STEM Space Engagement Gap
* **The Educational Challenge:** Complex aerospace concepts (electrolysis, thermodynamic cascades, orbital mechanics, planetary meteorology) are traditionally taught through dry, intimidating textbook formulas.
* **The Need:** A zero-barrier, cross-platform learning tool that teaches systems thinking: showing students how interconnected resource trade-offs govern survival on another world.
* **The Mission:** Connect the Artemis and Mars generation directly to NASA's active mission telemetry through hands-on decision-making.

---

## 🚀 SLIDE 3: The Solution – Dual-Core Architecture
1. **Mars Mission Control Simulator:**
   * Manage 7 closed-loop life-support subsystems (Power, O₂, Food, Water, Heat, Shielding, Integrity).
   * 4 escalating mission levels (Touchdown, Red Dust, Solar Storm, Apocalypse Protocol).
   * Reactive SVG digital twin schematic highlighting failing modules with cascading thermodynamic consequences.
2. **NASA Space Academy (8 Playable Physics Mini-Games):**
   * *Planet Explorer* (Astronomy), *Zero Gravity Challenge* (Newtonian EVA), *Rocket Builder* (Propulsion & TWR), *Mars Rover* (Jezero Geology), *Asteroid Defender* (DART Deflection), *Save The Astronaut* (ECLSS), *Gravity Lab* (Keplerian Orbits), and *Space Quiz Battle*.

---

## 🛰️ SLIDE 4: Authentic NASA Data & Science Provenance
* **Empirical Observations:** Real atmospheric telemetry from **Perseverance MEDA** and **Curiosity REMS** (pressure, surface temperatures, dust opacity $\tau$).
* **Life Support Benchmarks:** Solid-oxide electrolysis rates (6–12 g/h) calibrated from **NASA PDS MOXIE** operational data.
* **Planetary Defense & Space Weather:** **NASA DONKI** Coronal Mass Ejection modeling and **NASA DART** kinetic impactor physics.
* **Provenance Transparency:** Every metric clearly labeled as **OBSERVED**, **DERIVED**, or **SIMULATED**—never presenting synthetic values as real data.

---

## ⚙️ SLIDE 5: Full-Stack Engineering & AI Flight Copilot
* **Backend:** Python 3.10+ & Django 6.0 engine with deterministic turn-based state machines and SQLite replay logging.
* **Houston AI Flight Copilot:** Forward simulation tree evaluating candidate moves 1 Sol ahead; offline rule-based expert system + optional natural language LLM chat.
* **Predictive Survival Analytics:** Chart.js multi-Sol trajectory forecasts alerting students to resource bottlenecks before they occur.
* **Cockpit Audio Immersion:** Real-time procedural soundscapes via browser-native **Web Audio API** (Martian wind, sirens, Geiger clicks) and **Web Speech API** voice comms.

---

## 🎓 SLIDE 6: Age-Adaptive Learning & Global Impact
* **Adaptive Cognitive Calibration:**
  * *Cadet (8–9 yrs):* Visual cues, forgiving timers, plain-English subtitles (*"Make Breathing Air"*).
  * *Specialist (10–13 yrs):* Quantitative metrics, Astronomical Units, and thermodynamic freeze cascades.
  * *Commander (14–16 yrs):* Full realism, vis-viva orbital mechanics, and compounding crises.
* **Anti-Cheat Gamification:** 9 NASA STEM Badges, daily challenges, and printable Junior Commander Certificates with QR verification.
* **Earth Stewardship:** Reinforces circular economy, closed-loop recycling, and clean energy resilience for our home planet.
